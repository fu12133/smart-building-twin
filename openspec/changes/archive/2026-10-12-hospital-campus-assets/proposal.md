## Why
The user identified the project as a hospital and authorized using hospital-specific art assets. Generic office towers and commercial podiums do not communicate a hospital campus.

## What Changes
Import a legitimately downloaded hospital model with verified distribution rights, package the optimized model locally, and use it in the existing rotatable BuildingScene. Adapt Chinese names and demonstration floor/equipment semantics to hospital buildings. Preserve camera controls, floor bounds, GPU cleanup and GitHub Pages base paths. Keep the scene explicitly identified as a demonstration rather than the user's actual hospital BIM.

## Asset decision
Start with the free CC BY Hospital model by Er.B.Nijithkumar (Sketchfab ID 0012226ea07d4161b4d2c133a9b11448). Its official metadata reports 42,042 triangles and downloadable status. Actual download requires an authenticated official session; asset acquisition and inspection are prerequisites to declaring the change complete. No paid purchase or viewer-data extraction is part of this change.
