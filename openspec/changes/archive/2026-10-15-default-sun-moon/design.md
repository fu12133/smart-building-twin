# Design

Reuse SceneAtmosphere's owned sky texture and aligned directional light. A fixed low illustrative celestial direction and a lower initial/reset exterior camera expose sky above the source hospital on desktop and phone; neither minute updates nor weather/language changes move the camera. Celestial direction and moon appearance are display design, not verified ephemerides or lunar phase. Default daytime has warm sun/halo; night has a softly illuminated cratered moon and cool directional fill alongside hospital lights. Clouds/rain attenuate celestial brightness and direct lighting, retaining the user-requested default disc. Missing weather uses Shanghai clock day/night without fabricating readings. Explicit weather-off still restores the static preset. Existing optional sunny preview remains labeled non-live; no action is required for default sun/moon. Interiors keep existing neutral lighting and saved exterior pose. Resources remain bounded on software GPU, disposable and idle without precipitation.

Verification uses UI-only initial load at fixed day/night times, rendered celestial-core pixels and screenshots at desktop/768/390, weather attenuation and live readings, unchanged camera on updates/language, floor lighting and complete existing regression suite. The lower initial/reset camera is an intentional framing change, not a change to geometry or fit scale.
